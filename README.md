### 🎧 Half & Half Music Player
**Two people, two devices, one song, in sync.**
Peer-to-peer listening room: the host streams an audio file to a guest over a WebRTC DataChannel and keeps play / pause / seek aligned within milliseconds. Includes search & download of originals, an Android APK, and a single-file dashboard.

`React 18` `Vite` `Zustand` `WebRTC` `Web Audio API` `Node.js`

[Repo](https://github.com/Karry1123/Half-Half-Music-Player) · [Live demo](https://karry1123.github.io/Half-Half-Music-Player/dashboard.html)

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🀄 顶龙麻将 · DingLong Mahjong
**One rules engine, three ways to play.**
A web recreation of the DingLong mahjong table: PvE against AI, 2–4 player PvP over WebSocket, and endgame study mode, all sharing one scoring engine with EV / shanten / tile-danger analysis.

`Vue 3` `FastAPI` `Python 3.11` `WebSocket` `GitHub Actions`

[Repo](https://github.com/Karry1123/DingLongMahjong) · [Releases](https://github.com/Karry1123/DingLongMahjong/releases)
